THREE REMAINING MATERIAL RISKS

1. HIGH — Three-control eligibility still depends on evidence outside the team’s control.
A complete source census across three zones and retries, usable independent expected-work records, accepted C9 labels/cohorts and a real ServiceNow route may not be available. The design correctly refuses to turn this into a pass. That honesty protects quality but cannot replace completed-control count.
Required next action: turn first-session probes into recorded acceptance examples with real field mappings, permissions and source IDs. Timebox organizer resolution. The90-minute block should produce a decision and evidence samples, not be treated as a promise that every integration is solved. Set an explicit latest decision time for the three-control path; if accepted C9 input cannot be established then, plan and present the two-control ceiling honestly.

2. HIGH — RoleB is a delivery bottleneck, and the contingency still concentrates too much work.
RoleB owns benchmark construction, baseline/window execution, ServiceNow, query/export, release and the independently implemented rechecker. Meanwhile RoleA’s inline state machine can absorb the remainder of the event. Both are critical-path workloads, especially for a team starting fresh with Claude skills.
Concrete revision: after RoleA closes C7/C16 accounting, prioritize assistance on query/export/rechecker and case execution before inline admission. Keep the judge CLI as the only required UI. Treat admission as a bounded final increment only after a complete three-control bundle exists, rather than whenever A’s local tasks finish. Name artifact owners and make the first release bundle executable on Day1, so packaging does not become a final-night project. This is an effort-allocation correction, not a request for another component.

3. MEDIUM — Authorized breached cases and data export rights are release dependencies, not demo details.
The architecture now distinguishes actual breaches, successful prevention and copied-export challenges correctly. However, genuine C7 loss/drift degradation/overspend cases require accepted scenario provenance; a beautifully functioning denial or synthetic fixture cannot substitute automatically. Likewise, offline reproducibility needs the actual raw inputs to be permitted and available for export.
Concrete revision: obtain an explicit per-control case/provenance acceptance decision and inspect one sample export early, alongside threshold freeze. Record any missing/redacted critical source as a reproduction limitation. Verify actual source-reference resolution in a clean offline rehearsal. If no eligible breach or essential evidence export exists, keep the release NOT_READY for that claimed control rather than relabel a fixture as assessed evidence.

No fundamental contradiction found between local/cloud deployment, four transports, five operating modes, AgentCore versus the shared model gateway, or the six official artifacts. The remaining risks require workshop facts, effort allocation and executed acceptance evidence; additional architecture prose alone will not eliminate them.
