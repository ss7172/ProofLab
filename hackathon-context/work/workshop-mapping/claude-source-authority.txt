THE OBJECTIVE AND DEFINITION OF DONE
Masterclass claim C-FM-01: during fault resolution, three collaborating agents meet declared event-recording, drift and token-spend requirements. Build evidence-backed safeguards and tests against these three branches:
- 7 / AIA-LOG-001: automatic event recording; independent expected-event coverage and eligible consecutive-event gaps across declared scope.
- 9 / AIA-ARC-006: performance/drift against frozen baseline in comparable windows, monitoring availability and named-owner alerts.
- 16 / ACN-COST-001: per-run input plus output tokens across all three agents and every actual retry; complete reconciliation and dated cap/allowance.
At least one control completed end to end is required. Two completed controls rank ahead of one; three is the full set. A completed detector can truthfully report BREACHED. A displayed green verdict alone is not completion.
The six required steps (PDF p23): choose one or more of 7/9/16; declare metric/limit/exception tolerance/measurement basis/named owner with version/date before assessed runs; choose when to test (post-run floor, runtime bonus); build at permitted enforcement points with control/version/run bindings; build reproducible control tests/evaluators; demonstrate passing and breaching cases without moving thresholds and record gaps.
Done means a person outside the team can select a control and run/window and receive the answer with supporting records in two minutes (120 seconds). Query scope must retain complete relevant populations, not a favorable subset. An answer can be BREACHED or INDETERMINATE with reasons; completion of the claimed branch still needs its accepted end-to-end demonstration.
