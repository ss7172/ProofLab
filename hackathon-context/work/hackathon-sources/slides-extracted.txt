THRESHOLDSControl have no threshold range. You have to choose it.The law states an outcome and a reusable control fixes the shape of the answer. Only your own specification holds a number — and its date is what makes it a test.The lawthe legislature“An appropriate level of accuracy, declared in the instructions for use.”NO NUMBERThe library controlwhoever writes the libraryA metric exists, is justified against the intended purpose, and the achieved level is declared.STILL NO NUMBERYour specificationyouGroundedness on the fixed test set, between [0.85, 1], measured on data held out from the last four quarters.YOUR NUMBERThe test recordthe testerMeasured 0.81 on 12 March. Fail. Finding raised, owner assigned, re-test scheduled.THE ANSWERFOUR QUESTIONS FOR EVERY THRESHOLD RANGE1Why this metric?Tie it to the purpose and to the harm you are trying to avoid.2Why this value?A human baseline, a prior system, an industry reference. “It is what we achieved” is not an answer.3Measured on what?Name the dataset, the period, and how it was held out.4When does it stop holding?Work out the boundary conditions now, or you cannot disclose them later.A threshold range chosen after seeing the result is a description of what you got, not a test. The version date on your threshold document is how anybody tells the difference.11
