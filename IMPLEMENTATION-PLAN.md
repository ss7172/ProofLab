## 1. Goal and DONE

**The goal.** ProofLab is team iFabric's assurance system for the lab's three collaborating fault-resolution agents. It closes the judgment gap (our `ifabric-` agents gather evidence or escalate instead of guessing, and record why), completes C16, C7 and C9 end to end against a dated, versioned register, and hands over one Evidence Pack in which a stranger answers "was control X satisfied for run Y or window T1..T2?" in two minutes, plus the six auditor questions, from the pack alone [ARCH §1.1]. The whole solution:
- **Frontend:** Arth's Evidence Console (loopback, PACK and LIVE modes) as the judged dashboard, over our query engine, with Arth's ServiceNow panel embedded unchanged.
- **Backend:** the laptop hub: the orchestrating script, collectors, the one store (the platform ledger), the stitcher, the stateless evaluators, the query engine, the pack builder and the human-loop runner.
- **AI agents:** the `ifabric-` trio (customer, IT, network) on `nemotron-super-120b`, plus the 4th AgentCore runtime `ifabric-assurance-agent` (our one-file handler around Arth's unchanged analyser).
- **AWS:** AgentCore runtimes, `modaas-agw`, `aws/spans`, the evidence bucket (T1 write-once), CloudTrail and the AICT discovery trail.
- **ServiceNow:** incidents for findings, the Assurance agent's validated work note, the owner's response or silence, the T2 approval incident, AICT inventory.
- **Guardrails at every EP:** the fixed gateway guardrail (observed), in-agent rails that may block, Cedar edits on an existing ToolConfig, the C16 guard.

**DONE means every box below is ticked, submitted and rehearsed.** The build is phased (§4.1) and every phase ends with a submit: **Phase 1** (the minimum submission, target ~05:30) ticks the agents, C16 + C7 (C9's threshold in `v1` and an honest "not assessed yet" answer for C9 (G5)`. The G5 check itself is in 17B-06's insert (`--control C9 --run <N1's CID>), the minimal notify, the CLI and submit #1; **Phase 2** ticks ServiceNow, the Assurance agent, AICT, the app, C9 and submit #2; **Phase 3** ticks the adversarial set, the demo, the rehearsals and the final submit.
